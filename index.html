About the YSA

Educating youth. Building chapters. Fighting exploitation.

The Young Socialists of America is a Marxist-Leninist youth organization for people ages 14 to 24. Our goal is to educate youth, build socialist consciousness, and organize for a future socialist society led by the working class.

Through education, solidarity, and collective struggle, we work toward a society without exploitation. Building chapters is a core goal. We want the YSA to stand as a legitimate organization with members doing real work where they live.

The YSA is Marxist-Leninist in orientation. Members from other socialist tendencies are welcome as long as they come in good faith, open to debate, education, and collective learning. Leadership positions require a commitment to Marxism-Leninism.

Why the bison?

The bison represents strength, unity, and resistance.

It is an animal deeply tied to the history of the Americas and symbolizes survival against colonial-capitalist destruction. Its near-extermination reflected the violence of colonial expansion.

The bison stands for endurance, solidarity, and collective power.

Local action builds national power.

    Opposing fascist and reactionary organizing wherever it shows up in our communities.

    Protecting our communities, especially the people most targeted by repression and reactionary violence.

Who can run for council

To run for or be appointed to council, a member must:

  1. Be at least 16 years old.
  2. Have been a YSA member for at least two months.
  3. Identify as a Marxist-Leninist and uphold democratic centralism.
  4. Have prior organizing experience.
  5. Have a clean disciplinary record for the past three months.
  6. Be an active member of the organization.
  7. Demonstrate political literacy, including a basic understanding of Marxist-Leninist theory and organizational principles such as democratic centralism.
  8. Be willing to follow and uphold council decisions and organizational discipline.
  9. Be able to commit time to council duties, including attending meetings and completing assigned responsibilities.

How elections work

Election rules continue the same numbered list.

  1. Regular elections for council positions take place every December.

    If a council member steps down or a seat becomes vacant before then, a special election will be held as soon as possible to fill it.

  2. Uncontested seats.

    If only one eligible candidate runs for a council position, they receive the position by default without a formal vote.

  3. Sitting council members do not vote.

    No sitting member of council may vote in any council election, regular or special. This ensures impartiality and prevents conflicts of interest in the selection of new council members.

  4. Derivative tendencies.

    Members who identify with Marxist-Leninist derivative tendencies, such as Marxist-Leninist-Maoist (MLM) or Marxist-Leninist Hoxhaist (MLH), may run for or serve on council, provided their beliefs do not contradict core orthodox Marxist-Leninist principles.

  5. Vote justification requirement.

    All votes in council elections must include at least one sentence explaining the reasoning behind the choice. It can be brief, but it must clearly state the voter’s justification, even something as simple as preferring one candidate’s policies, experience, or positions over the others.

FAQ

Questions we get a lot

New to Marxism-Leninism or to the YSA? Start here. For more, study the YSA Charter and Membership Handbook alongside Marxist theory and discussions with comrades.

What is Marxism-Leninism?

Marxism-Leninism, often shortened to ML, is a term popularized by Joseph Stalin to describe the political and philosophical ideas of Karl Marx and Friedrich Engels, which were developed and applied to revolutionary practice by Vladimir Lenin and the Bolshevik Party.

It is both a scientific analysis of capitalism and a guide to building socialism through organized working-class power.

What is dialectical materialism?

Dialectical materialism is the worldview of Marxism-Leninism.

It is called dialectical because it studies change through contradictions and motion. It is materialist because it understands reality through material conditions rather than ideas alone.

Historical materialism is the application of dialectical materialism to society and history. It explains how systems like slavery, feudalism, and capitalism arise and change based on economic conditions and class struggle.

What is private property?

When Marxists speak about private property, we do not mean personal belongings. Personal belongings are personal property.

Personal property exists for use value. A toothbrush is used to brush teeth. A coat is used to stay warm.

Private property is property used to produce profit through labor exploitation. Factories, large farms, corporations, and rental empires exist not to meet needs but to generate profit for owners.

How do I get into Marxism-Leninism?

Study theory. Organize with other socialists. Participate in political education.

Marxism-Leninism is learned through collective study and practical organizing. Reading groups, discussions, and disciplined action help build understanding.

Have actually existing socialist states succeeded?

In many ways, yes.

Socialist states industrialized poor nations, expanded literacy, defeated fascism, and lifted millions out of extreme poverty.

They faced difficulties and contradictions, but many common accusations about them lack nuance or rely on propaganda rather than historical context.

Do we accept other tendencies?

The organization is Marxist-Leninist in orientation.

People from other ideological backgrounds may join and participate, but leadership positions require commitment to Marxism-Leninism and willingness to study and develop within it. Other tendencies are not eligible for council positions or for being electoral candidates.

Are there tendencies we do not accept?

Reactionary politics, fascism, and ideologies that defend capitalism or imperialism are not accepted.

The YSA opposes systems that exploit the working class or oppressed peoples.

What is the class struggle?

Class struggle is the contradiction between those who control production and those who do the work.

Under capitalism, this is the struggle between the bourgeoisie and the proletariat. The bourgeoisie seek profit. Workers seek survival and dignity.

Profit comes from surplus value taken from workers. This contradiction drives strikes, protests, and revolutions throughout history.

What is revisionism?

Revisionism is the distortion or weakening of Marxism-Leninism.

It replaces revolutionary struggle with reformism and compromises with capitalism. It often leads to the restoration of capitalist systems.

Marxist-Leninists oppose revisionism to preserve the revolutionary line.

What is imperialism?

Imperialism is the highest stage of capitalism.

It is a global system of monopoly where finance capital and industrial capital dominate foreign markets through direct or indirect control. Colonies, economic pressure, sanctions, and political intervention are tools of imperialism.

It exploits workers internationally and deepens inequality between nations.

What does it mean to be reactionary, right-wing, or liberal?

Reactionary politics defend existing systems of exploitation.

Right-wing ideology openly protects capitalism and hierarchy. Liberalism often claims to oppose injustice while preserving the capitalist structures that create it.

Marxism-Leninism seeks liberation beyond capitalism itself.

Isn’t Marxism-Leninism authoritarian?

Every state has authority. Laws. Police. Courts. Leadership.

The question is who that authority serves.

Capitalist states protect property. Socialist states protect people.

Liberals often use the term authoritarian to convey a negative message about socialist nations. Any nation with laws and leadership is authoritarian by definition. Being authoritarian is not an inherently negative thing.

What is a reactionary?

In Marxist-Leninist theory, a reactionary is any individual, group, or system that actively seeks to preserve or restore oppressive social, economic, and political hierarchies. Reactionaries resist revolutionary change because it threatens their power or privilege. They are opposed to the liberation of the working class, oppressed nations, and marginalized people.

Reactionaries do not just exist in government or formal politics. They are found in social norms, institutions, and everyday behaviors that reinforce oppression. Mao called them “paper tigers” because, while they may look powerful, they ultimately depend on the subjugation of the masses and can be overthrown through collective struggle.

Essentially, a reactionary is anyone who actively or passively tries to stop social liberation. From misogynists to imperialist powers, their “power” depends on keeping people divided and oppressed. Theory teaches us that when people are organized, educated, and united, even the most terrifying reactionaries are just paper tigers.

What is decolonization?

Decolonization is the conscious, organized resistance to colonial systems that exploit our land, labor, and culture. Its goal is to dismantle colonial power and achieve real Indigenous liberation and self-determination.

What is self-determination?

Self-determination is the right of oppressed nations and peoples to control their own political future, culture, land, and economy without outside domination. It means communities deciding their path for themselves, not having it imposed by empires, corporations, or colonial states.

While Marx supported specific national liberation struggles as part of the class struggle, it was Lenin who made the right to self-determination, up to and including secession, a core demand of the communist movement. He argued this was essential because unity between workers of different nations must be voluntary and based on trust, not coercion. Stalin later provided a classic definition of a nation, a historically stable community of language, territory, economic life, and psychological makeup, to help identify which groups this right applies to.

In ML ideology, this is a democratic right, not a tactical prescription. While communists defend the right to secede, they may politically argue against it in a specific context if breaking into small, weak states would leave workers more vulnerable to imperialism. The ultimate goal is not to fragment the world into countless tiny nation-states, but to end national oppression so that nations can enter into a free and voluntary union with their neighbors. This principle extends beyond politics to actively developing the economies and cultures of formerly oppressed groups, working to dissolve the material inequalities that fuel national antagonism in the first place.

Server rules

Rules and expectations

These rules apply to everyone in the YSA Discord server. Read them before you join.

1Zero tolerance for hate or discrimination.

Any form of racism, antisemitism, Islamophobia, anti-Indigenous hate, homophobia, transphobia, misogyny, ableism, or similar bigotry will result in an instant and permanent ban. No warnings.

2Stay in the appropriate channels.

Keep casual or joking content in informal channels and serious political or organizational discussion in designated spaces. Repeated failure to do so will result in a warning or timeout.

3Activity is required.

Members are expected to participate to some extent. Extended inactivity without explanation may result in a kick to keep the server functional and organized.

4Affiliation with incompatible organizations and ideologies.

The YSA is a Marxist-Leninist organization. Membership is reserved for individuals whose political views are compatible with ML or who are genuinely open to good-faith study, criticism, and ideological development within the organization.

Membership or active involvement in organizations, parties, or ideological tendencies deemed incompatible with the YSA’s mission may result in removal at the discretion of council. This includes, but is not limited to, the ACP, Nazbols, anarchists, Trotskyists, the DSA and similar liberal electoral organizations, Hoxhaists, Gonzaloids, Left Communists, and Communizers. Applicants who actively identify with these organizations or ideologies will likely not be admitted to the YSA, and existing members who maintain such affiliations may be removed.

The YSA encourages discussion and engagement with differing socialist perspectives only when those perspectives are not fundamental deviations from ML and when participants engage in good faith with a willingness to learn, accept criticism, and contribute constructively to the organization.

5All discussion must remain legal and comply with Discord TOS.

Punishment depends on the nature and severity of the violation.

6Moderation authority.

Council members and deputies act as moderators and administrators. Their decisions carry final authority in most cases. Follow their guidance.

7Reporting abuse of power.

If an admin or moderator is found abusing their authority, report it to a council member or the founder immediately.

8Conduct in serious discussion.

Keep debates formal, principled, and respectful. Disagreement is encouraged. Personal insults are not. If someone is advocating racism, homophobia, misogyny, or similar bigotry, report them instead of engaging. Violations may result in a timeout.

9Chapter building is a core goal.

Our objective is to establish the YSA as a legitimate organization. Members are encouraged to work toward building or supporting chapters whenever possible. Our password is organized, not outraged.

10Mutual respect and unity.

Treat fellow members with respect. We are part of the same movement and should act in ways that strengthen collective unity and discipline.

11Ideological orientation.

The YSA is primarily a Marxist-Leninist organization. Other socialist tendencies are welcome so long as members are open to good-faith debate, education, and collective learning. Sectarianism and bad-faith disruption will not be tolerated.

12Age requirement.

Membership is restricted to individuals aged 14 to 24. No exceptions.

13No member poaching.

Poaching members is strictly prohibited and will result in an immediate and permanent ban. This includes DMing members to recruit them into another organization without explicit council approval.

Poaching is defined as members or leaders of one organization joining another organization for the primary purpose of taking its members.

14No active employment in law enforcement or the military.

Active employment in any law enforcement agency or military force is strictly prohibited and will result in removal. These institutions exist to enforce class domination and imperialism.

Prior service is permitted only if the individual openly acknowledges that participation as a mistake and recognizes those institutions and roles as class traitors and tools of imperialist violence. Apologia, glorification, or defense of police or military institutions will not be tolerated.

This organization stands in opposition to state violence and repression. There is no neutrality on this issue.

15Rule expansion.

Additional rules may be established as necessary to protect the organization, its members, and its mission.

16NSFW content.

Any content with gore, sexual activities, and anything else that may make others uncomfortable is prohibited.

17Ban on generative AI usage.

The use of generative AI in any form is prohibited. This includes tools such as ChatGPT, Grok, Gemini, Meta AI, DeepSeek, or similar systems.

  • No AI generated writing.
  • No AI generated images.
  • No AI assisted drafts or research passed off as your own work.

Generative AI is environmentally destructive, resource intensive, and contradicts the principles of our organization. Its use undermines collective discipline and revolutionary integrity.

If a member is caught using AI they will receive one warning. If caught again it will result in an immediate and permanent ban.

18Prohibition on praise or rehabilitation of reactionary figures.

Any praise, defense, or positive remarks of figures such as Pol Pot, Benito Mussolini, Heinrich Himmler, Adolf Hitler, Andrew Jackson, Donald Trump, Barack Obama, Christopher Columbus, or similar fascist, colonial, genocidal, or reactionary figures will be treated the same as affiliation with harmful organizations or ideologies under Rule 4.

Fascists, colonizers, imperial agents, and their defenders stand directly against revolutionary struggle. Attempting to rehabilitate them, glorify them, or sing apologia for their actions undermines our principles and harms the organization.

Violations may result in removal at council discretion.

19No “Schrödinger’s Douchebag” behavior.

A Schrödinger’s douchebag is someone who makes offensive, inflammatory, or harmful remarks and then claims they were “just joking” depending on how people react. This behavior is commonly used by reactionaries to push hateful rhetoric while avoiding accountability.

Examples include making racist, homophobic, sexist, or otherwise harmful remarks and then claiming people are “too sensitive” when called out, hiding behind “dark humor,” or attempting to ragebait discussions about serious issues that affect real people.

This type of behavior is disruptive, dishonest, and harmful to the organization.

If a member engages in this behavior they may be muted while the council reviews the situation, or they will be kicked outright. A kick under this rule may include the possibility of returning in the future only if genuine growth and a sincere apology are demonstrated.

20No slur usage under any circumstances.

Expanding on Rule 1, the use of slurs or explicit hate speech is strictly prohibited in this server under any circumstances.

This includes attempting to reclaim slurs. While some individuals or communities may reclaim certain terms, this server cannot allow that usage due to the strict TOS enforced by Discord. The use of slurs can result in accounts being banned or the entire server being removed.

Additionally, the open use of these terms creates opportunities for bad faith actors to screenshot and spread them publicly in an attempt to discredit the organization. Many people outside the community will not understand the context of reclamation, which puts both members and the organization at large at risk.

Ignorance will not be accepted as an excuse. Violating this rule will result in an immediate kick or ban.

21Restriction on mass pings.

The use of mass pings such as @everyone or @here is strictly prohibited unless absolutely necessary.

Mass pings may only be used with explicit council approval or in urgent situations such as server raids, compromised accounts, security threats, or important safety announcements regarding members.

Unnecessary or unauthorized use of mass pings is disruptive and will not be tolerated. Violations will result in a warning and a week-long timeout, and repeated offenses may lead to further disciplinary action such as kicks or bans.

22Zero tolerance for misogynistic terminology.

The use of misogynistic language is strictly prohibited. This includes terms such as “b*tch” or similar degrading or gender based insults.

Because many people are unaware or dismissive of the misogynistic nature of this language, this rule is being stated clearly and explicitly. There is no ambiguity.

Using misogynistic terminology undermines the safety, respect, and unity of the organization and will not be tolerated. Violations will result in a warning or timeout, with repeated offenses leading to stronger disciplinary action.

23Prohibition on misuse of Jeffrey Epstein references and jokes.

Any jokes, memes, or casual use of Epstein as a punchline, insult, or shorthand label are strictly prohibited.

This includes using “Epstein” to mock, degrade, or label individuals in a non-serious or performative way. Such usage trivializes sexual abuse and trafficking and will be treated as harassment or inappropriate conduct.

Contextual comparisons involving sexual abuse or exploitation are permitted only when they are backed by credible evidence, relevant to the discussion, and used to accurately describe behavior rather than as a meme or insult. In such cases, references to Epstein or similar figures must be clearly factual in intent and not used as a rhetorical shortcut.

This rule is intended to prevent the normalization of abuse-related figures as jokes while preserving the ability to discuss and name patterns of sexual violence responsibly.

Violations will be treated the same as Rule 18 and Rule 4, which may lead to removal at council discretion.

24Prohibition on punishment evasion and alt account misuse.

Alternative accounts are allowed within the server, but they cannot be used to evade moderation actions.

Any attempt to use an alternate account to bypass punishment is prohibited. This includes, but is not limited to, ban evasion, using an alt to speak while your primary account is muted, or otherwise using alternate accounts to sidestep restrictions placed on your main account.

Moderation actions apply to the user, not just the account. Violations of this rule may result in further action being taken across all associated accounts.

Failure to follow these rules undermines the organization and will be dealt with accordingly.
